Opportunity

Micro-Tier Overdelivers on CPE

Ava Patel and Yuki Tanaka (micro-tier) achieved $0.06 CPE — half the mega-tier cost. While their absolute reach was lower, engagement rate (11-12%) was significantly higher. Recommendation: Expand micro-tier roster from 2 to 8-10 creators for always-on content.

Action

YouTube Underindexed — Test More

YouTube only contributed 8% of total impressions but had the highest save rate (8.4%), suggesting strong purchase intent. The long-form review format ("Does Ceramide Serum Work?") resonated deeply with audiences. Recommendation: Add 2-3 dedicated YouTube creators in next campaign.
