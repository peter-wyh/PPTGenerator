09 / Strategic Insights

Key Findings & Recommendations

Data-driven insights from comparing all three partner types.

Winner

Content Sites Drive Highest GMV

GlowGuide alone generated $127K GMV — the single highest across all 14 partners. Content sites contributed 32% of total GMV despite being only 29% of partners.

Their SEO-driven long-tail traffic sustained conversions well beyond the campaign period, making them the most cost-efficient partner type.

  • Lowest CAC ($3.7/order vs $7.7 for creators)
  • Highest order volume (1,236 orders)
  • 42% of orders came from organic search
Opportunity

Communities Offer Best Engagement

Skincare Junkies achieved 20% engagement rate — 3× higher than the average creator. AMA sessions drove 378 orders at the lowest cost-per-order.

  • Group buy format converted 2.1% of members
  • Highest repeat purchase rate (34%)
  • Members self-amplify via organic word-of-mouth
  • Risk: requires dedicated community management
Action

Creator + Content Site = Best Combo

Campaigns using both creators and content sites saw 2.3× higher total GMV than creator-only campaigns. Creators generate initial awareness; content sites capture high-intent search traffic from that awareness.

  • Day 1-14: Creators launch (awareness burst)
  • Day 15-60: Content sites capture SEO demand
  • Day 30+: Communities drive repeat purchases
  • Recommended budget split: 40/35/25
