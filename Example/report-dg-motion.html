3.6 · Actionable Insights

Top Performers

Leo Sato drove $18,097 in revenue (34% of total) on 395 attributed orders — the campaign's revenue anchor. Tom Reyes delivered the strongest efficiency at ROAS 9.16 with the highest CVR (3.25%); scale his content volume next flight.

Stable Growth After Ramp

From Mar 15 onward, daily revenue stepped up from a ~$750 baseline to a sustained $1,000–$2,181 band for the remainder of the flight, peaking at $2,181.09 on Mar 22. Post-ramp decay was mild — a sign of durable seeding impact.

Platform Mix

YouTube publishers generated 21,941 clicks (59.6%) and $32,201 in sales — the conversion backbone. Douyin and TikTok added incremental scale at comparable CVR (3.08%–3.25%), validating the cross-platform seeding structure.

Watch: High Traffic, Flat CVR

Click-to-order conversion held within a tight 3.08%–3.26% band across publishers. Stella Zhou's Douyin traffic converted lowest (3.08%) despite a 12.0% engagement rate — review the landing path for that channel to unlock upside.
