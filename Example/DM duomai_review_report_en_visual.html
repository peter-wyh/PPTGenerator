  • Invest in Mega/Macro influencers on Instagram and TikTok. Pilot a few high-impact Mega creators on TikTok before scaling.

Key Takeaways

Challenge → Strategy

Challenge

  • Lack of sustained CPA advantage relative to competitors, leading to weak sales in most years.
  • Severely underinvested social presence: lowest post volume, buzz, and content diversity across platforms.
  • Over-reliance on low-tier influencers; Mega and Macro tiers largely untapped.
  • Generic content fails to differentiate the brand.

Strategy

  • Secure higher, stable CPA: Re-engage publishers and drive conversions.
  • Add flat-fee models to unlock premium placements, exclusive content sites, and top-tier influencers across Instagram and TikTok (following TickPick's low-post, high-buzz model).
  • Adopt a guaranteed volume model: With committed budget, use flat fee to secure stable monthly exposure and sales.
  • Leverage REMAi data: Screen influencers, provide smart targeting strategies, and optimize campaign timing.
